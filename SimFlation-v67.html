Sector ownership targetsEnergy 0%Current public stakes and policy targets will appear here.

Each productive sector now keeps its own ownership target. The model moves firms in every sector towards their stored target each month, so changing the sector selector no longer pauses another sector's nationalisation or privatisation. Raising a target buys equity from shareholders; lowering it sells public stakes. The mandate changes how publicly owned firms balance returns, capacity and service resilience.

When the fiscal stabiliser is on, cash above the reserve target, recent fiscal surpluses and limited market financing can support demand and temporary service-sector jobs in weak periods. The job-response percentage is the share of unemployment above an 8% labour-force floor the stabiliser attempts to cover, subject to available funding. The chosen debt ceiling still governs normal borrowing.

Manual mode keeps the chosen policy rate fixed. The automatic rule responds to inflation, unemployment, real growth and an internally estimated neutral rate.

Important structural limitations

There are five productive sectors, a stylised housing stock, one aggregate foreign sector and a scaled individual population (1,000 people at reset). People are modelled individually for age, education, employment, retirement, birth and death, while housing, consumption and most wealth remain household-level. Opposition party-leader names are fictional and generated deterministically from the random seed. Public-service budgets fund individual jobs and departmental output, while individual institutions remain aggregated. Geography, planning delays, securities markets, family relationships and detailed bank regulation remain simplified. The built-in multi-seed audit should be used before treating an outcome as robust. The model is designed for experimentation, not forecasting.

Key fixed assumptions and thresholds
Bank distress
Resolution can follow persistent insolvency, severe negative equity or prolonged undercapitalisation. A recapitalisation succeeds only when the bank is solvent and meets the regulatory capital floor afterwards.
Company failure
Firms can fail after sustained losses, liquidity collapse, excessive leverage or prolonged operating distress. Financial failure now passes through a heterogeneous administration period, and viable productive capacity can transfer through receivership rather than disappearing instantly. Poor outcomes are retained when the accounting remains valid.
Constitutional authority
Regime type, monarchy or republic, election status, electoral system, term length, devolution and the second chamber change only when the user applies a reform. Managed elections guarantee the incumbent a governing majority while preserving plausible opposition representation, personalist rulers remain in office until death and are succeeded by a dynastic heir, and a ruling monarch directly exercises executive power.
Election integrity
Every regional allocation must respect territorial eligibility and every completed Commons allocation must match the population-linked seat count established by the latest census.
Invariant enforcement
Balance-sheet, relationship, guardian, identifier and Parliament checks run after each month. A failed check pauses the live simulation and records the exact month. Fast-run controls use a time budget and yield to the browser regularly so the interface remains responsive. Unexpected JavaScript errors halt safely and remain exportable for diagnosis. Older monthly records are compacted while annual rich-detail snapshots are retained to limit long-run memory growth. Persistent owner-home vacancy can feed rental supply, sovereign recovery after repeated defaults is continuous rather than threshold-based, and firm failure timing includes stable firm-specific resilience, persistent insolvency and staggered administration so common shocks do not mechanically liquidate whole sectors in the same month. Firms now retain an unconstrained desired-staff signal above their current staffing cap, can expand that cap after sustained financially viable labour demand, and high unemployment can encourage additional entry only where incumbent profitability, capacity pressure or unmet demand makes entry commercially plausible.
